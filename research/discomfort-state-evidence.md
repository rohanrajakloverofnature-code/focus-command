# Discomfort State — Research Note

## Purpose

This note supports adding **Discomfort** as a neutral, non-clinical Shadow Gate state. It must not diagnose the user or represent a medical/mental-health feature.

## Relevant evidence

Research on affect labeling examines the effects of briefly naming an emotional state; the additional Shadow Gate option uses this mechanism only as a short self-report choice, followed by an entirely voluntary concrete action.[1]

Work on psychological flexibility and experiential avoidance distinguishes moving away from an unpleasant internal experience from choosing an action aligned with a personally meaningful goal. Shadow Gate should therefore avoid pressure, reassurance claims, or exposure language. The appropriate translation is: name `Discomfort`, choose an optional small doorway, and retain the unconditional right to close the sheet.[2]

## Safe product framing

- Label: **Discomfort**.
- Plain-language helper: **“Starting feels unpleasant right now.”**
- Sample doorways: **“Take one slow breath, then open the material.”**, **“Touch the first tool; no work is required yet.”**, and **“Place the page in front of you, then choose again.”**
- No diagnosis, treatment, claim of emotional reduction, compulsory persistence, score, or logged record when the user exits.
