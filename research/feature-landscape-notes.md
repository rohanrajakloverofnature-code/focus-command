# Focus Command Feature Landscape Notes

## Current-product pattern observed

- Current gamified focus products commonly combine timers, streaks, virtual rewards, accountability, and lightweight social play. These are established mechanics rather than novel differentiation. One reviewed example was Focumon, which presents a multiplayer gamified focus experience: <https://www.focumon.com/>.

## Research directions retained

- A 2024 paper argues that metacognitive reflection can shift learners' perspective and support lifelong learning. This supports features that help a user recognize how they work rather than merely counting focus minutes: <https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11368986/>.
- Search results on digital-wellbeing interventions and attention recovery support adaptive, contextual prompts rather than generic timers. The ideas proposed will remain user-controlled, offline-first, and non-diagnostic.

## Product-design implication

Focus Command should avoid treating points, streaks, a timer, or a virtual pet as unique. Its strongest differentiation is turning personal focus history into an interpretable RPG journey: a user-owned world that learns patterns, preserves meaningful evidence, and helps the player design their next attempt without surveillance or a cloud dependency.
