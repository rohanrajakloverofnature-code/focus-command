# Focus Command Sound Sources

The built-in feedback sound library is being refreshed with compact royalty-free sound effects from [Pixabay Sound Effects](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/), subject to the Pixabay Content License.

| App role | Candidate source | Intended use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tap / click | [Casual Click Pop UI 8](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-casual-click-pop-ui-8-262125/) | Short button and control feedback. |
| Mission win | [Gaming victory](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/gaming-victory-464016/) | Mission-completion confirmation. |
| Notification | Pixabay [UI sound effects](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/ui/) selection | Brief positive reminder alert. |
| Extended feedback | [Victory Chime](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/musical-victory-chime-366449/) or a selected level-up cue | Level, title, combo, and achievement celebrations. |

The in-app settings also let each player select a local MP3, M4A, AAC, or WAV file from their device for any sound role. This provides a user-controlled alternative to the bundled library.

The selected [Gaming victory](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/gaming-victory-464016/) cue is a compact four-second game-victory effect suitable for a mission completion or milestone celebration. Pixabay’s page presents it as free for use under the Pixabay Content License. The page does not expose a static MP3 source through its visible media elements, so the app also includes device-file selection for users who want to assign their own downloaded or licensed sound effect.
