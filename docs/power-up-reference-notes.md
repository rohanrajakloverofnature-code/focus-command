# Cinematic Power-Up Reference Notes

## Approved interaction boundary

The existing Home character/avatar remains the trigger. The existing title-achievement presentation must continue to reflect the current title and level, with no unrelated layout, navigation, color, typography, or gameplay changes.

## Primary sequence beats from the supplied reference

| Beat | Timing | Required visual language | Coordinated sound intent |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Activation | 0–1 s | Sharp starburst, avatar breaks from its circular constraint, controlled push-in | Short activation whoosh/chime |
| Build-up | 1–3 s | Darkened background, energy ring, rising particles, steady camera focus | Low rising energy bed |
| Transformation | 3–5 s | Bottom-up equipment/materialization reveal with a luminous trace | Scanning/assembly cue |
| Impact | 5–6 s | Brief impact flash and a small, deliberate shake | Single heavy impact with metallic reveal accent |
| Resolution | 6–9 s | Strong final pose, fading energy trails, smooth return to the unchanged Home view | Resolving final cue |

## Engineering safeguards

The sequence must be data-driven by existing title and current level, never stack under repeated taps, stop sounds and clear overlays on dismissal/unmount, preserve the source character for each title, and use compact native animation primitives rather than large replacement videos or a new game engine.
