# India Territory Map Data Attribution

Focus Command’s detailed India boundary is prepared from the simplified India GeoJSON available in the [`nswamy14/geoJson` repository](https://github.com/nswamy14/geoJson/blob/master/india.simplified.geo.json), which is licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/nswamy14/geoJson).

The map is a stylized in-app territory visualization. Subject territories are generated from the player’s own mission and revision records; they are not administrative or political boundaries.

A larger complementary boundary source is the [DataMeet India Maps repository](https://github.com/datameet/maps/blob/master/Country/india-composite.geojson), whose published data guidance requests attribution under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
